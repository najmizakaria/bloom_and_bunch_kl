CHAPTER  I  
INTRODUCTION  
 
1.1  PROJECT  BACKGROUND  
 In  recent  years,  online  shopping  has  become  an  essential  part  of  modern  consumer  
behaviour,
 
especially
 
in
 
the
 
gifting
 
industry.
 
Flowers
 
remain
 
one
 
of
 
the
 
most
 
popular
 
gifts
 
for
 
occasions
 
such
 
as
 
graduations,
 
weddings,
 
birthdays,
 
anniversaries,
 
and
 
corporate
 
events”
 (Etheredge  et  al.,  2025).   However,  many  small  and  medium-sized  flower  businesses  still  rely  heavily  on  manual  ordering  methods  such  as  WhatsApp  messages,  phone  calls,  or  walk-in  
customers.
 
These
 
methods
 
often
 
lead
 
to
 
miscommunication,
 
limited
 
customization
 
options,
 
and
 
inefficiencies
 
in
 
managing
 
orders
 
and
 
deliveries.
 
 
Most  existing  flower  mobile  application  offer  only  fixed  bouquet  designs  with  limited  
flexibility.
  
Customers
 
who
 
require
 
personalized
 
bouquets
 
usually
 
need
 
repeated
 communication  with  staff,  increasing  the  risk  of  misunderstanding  (Assaf,  2024).  In  addition,  the  lack  of  delivery  tracking  and  scheduling  flexibility  reduces  customer  confidence  (Dwivedi  &  Dey,  2025).  
Therefore,  this  project  proposes  the  development  of  a  mobile-based  custom  floral  
ordering
 
system.The
 
system
 
allows
 
customers
 
to
 
design
 
their
 
own
 
bouquets,
 
select
 
delivery
 
options,
 
and
 
track
 
orders
 
in
 
real
 
time.
 
For  our  FYP,  we  decided  to  work  with  Bloom  &  Bunch  Kuala  Lumpur  
(bloomandbunch.kl)
 
as
 
our
 
stakeholder
 
to
 
gain
 
insight
 
into
 
our
 
project.

 Although  online  flower  purchasing  is  becoming  more  popular,  customers  and  flower  
businesses

Many

on

WhatsApp,

The

been

               1.2.1  Limited  Bouquet  Customization  Online.                           1.2.1.1  Most  existing  flower  ordering  platforms  only  offer  pre-designed            
bouquets
 
with
 
minimal
 
customization
 
options.
 
                          1.2.1.2  Customers  are  unable  to  freely  choose  flower  types,  colours,  wrapping  
styles,
 
or
 
additional
 
gifts
 
based
 
on
 
their
 
personal
 
preferences.
 
                           1.2.1.3  This  limitation  prevents  customers  from  fully  expressing  their  
intentions
 
and
 
emotions
 
through
 
customized
 
floral
 
arrangements,
 
leading
 
to
 
dissatisfaction
 
(Hasdyna
 
et
 
al.,
 
2024).

=== PAGE 16 ===
 
1.2.2  Inefficient  Communication  Between  Customer  and  Florist   
(Astuti  et  al.,  2025) 
1.2.2.1  Many  flower  orders  are  handled  through  manual  
 
communication  channels  such  as  messaging  applications  
or  phone  calls.   
1.2.2.2  Repeated  explanations  are  often  required  to  clarify  
bouquet  design,  pricing,  and  delivery  details.   
1.2.2.3  This  process  increases  the  risk  of  miscommunication,  
order  errors,  and  unnecessary  workload.  
3  

=== PAGE 17 ===
 
1.2.3  Lack  of  Delivery  Tracking  and  Scheduling  Flexibility  (Sun  et  al.,  2023) 
1.2.3.1  Most  flower  ordering  methods  do  not  provide  real-time  
delivery  tracking  or  clear  delivery  status  updates.  
1.2.3.2  Customers  are  often  uncertain  about  the  delivery  
progress  and  estimated  arrival  time  of  their  orders.   
1.2.3.3  Limited  options  for  same-day  delivery  or  future  date  
booking  reduce  convenience,  especially  during  important  
occasions  such  as  weddings  and  graduations.  
4

   PROBLEM  ENCOUNTERED  COUNTERMEASURE  
Customers  experienced  difficulty  visualizing  customized  bouquet  arrangements  before  placing  orders.  This  caused  uncertainty  during  the  ordering  process.

A  structured  online  ordering  system  was  developed  to  organize  customization  requests,  delivery  information,  and  customer  instructions  directly  within  the  mobile  app.   
Customers  were  unable  to  monitor  delivery  progress  and  estimated  arrival  time  using  traditional  flower  ordering  methods
